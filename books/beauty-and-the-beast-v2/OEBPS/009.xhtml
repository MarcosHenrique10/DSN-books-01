When they reached a fork in the road, Maurice had Phillipe
 take a shortcut.

But after a while, it started to get dark, and a gloomy mist filled
 the air. Tree branches towered over their heads as wolves howled
 in the distance.

“This can’t be right,” Maurice said.

Without warning, a swarm of bats flew out from a tree and
 scared Phillipe! The poor horse threw Maurice and bolted, leaving
 him alone in the forest.
