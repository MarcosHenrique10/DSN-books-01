Just as a wolf lunged at Belle, a powerful claw reached out and
 saved her. It was the Beast! He stood over Belle, ready to protect
 her at any cost.

The wolves attacked, but the Beast fought them off. He was
 badly injured. Looking at Belle with sorry eyes, he collapsed into
 the snow.

Belle knew she couldn’t leave him there, so with Phillipe’s help,
 they took the Beast back to the castle.
