The Beast was badly injured. “At least I got to see you one
 last time,” he said, bringing his paw to Belle’s cheek. Then he
 closed his eyes and sank to the ground.

“No! Please don’t leave me!” cried Belle. “I love you!”

Inside the castle, the enchanted objects watched as the last
 rose petal fell. It was too late to break the spell.

But then magical streaks of light began to fill the sky.
