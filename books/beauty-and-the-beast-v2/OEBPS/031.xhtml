“Never!” Belle cried out. “My father’s not crazy! I can prove it.”
 She grabbed the enchanted mirror and showed the Beast to the
 villagers. “He’s really kind and gentle,” she told them. “He’s my
 friend.”

But no one would listen.

“We’ll rid the village of this beast,” Gaston said. Then Gaston
 and the villagers locked Belle and Maurice in the cottage and went
 to find the Beast.
