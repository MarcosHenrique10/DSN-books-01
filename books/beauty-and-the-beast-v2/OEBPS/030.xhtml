Belle found her father and brought him back to the cottage. He
 was surprised that the Beast had been kind enough to let her go.

Suddenly, someone knocked on the cottage door. The
 townspeople had come to take her father away. They thought
 Maurice was crazy for believing in a ferocious beast.

Gaston grabbed Belle’s arm. “I might be able to clear up this
 little misunderstanding if . . . you marry me,” he said.
