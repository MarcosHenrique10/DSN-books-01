After dinner, Belle couldn’t possibly think of sleeping. It was
 her first time in an enchanted castle, so Cogsworth gave her a tour.

But when he wasn’t looking, Belle climbed the steps to the
 forbidden West Wing. At the end of a hallway, she found herself in
 a dark room. A torn portrait of a handsome man hung on the wall.
 Nearby, a glowing rose wilted in a glass case.
