Suddenly, a hideous beast stormed in, growling from the
 shadows of the room. “So, you’ve come to stare at the Beast, have
 you?” he said, glaring at the intruder.

“Please, I meant no harm!” said Maurice. He was so scared he
 could barely speak. “I just need a place to stay.”

But the Beast wouldn’t listen. Roaring angrily, he grabbed
 Maurice and locked him in the castle tower.
