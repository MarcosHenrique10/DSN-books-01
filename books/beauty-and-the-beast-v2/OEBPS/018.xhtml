At the castle, the Beast showed Belle to her new room. He
 explained she could go anywhere—except for the West Wing.
 When she asked why, he lost his temper.

Belle didn’t ask the Beast any more questions after that. She
 even refused to eat dinner with him that night. “I’ve lost my father,
 my dreams, everything,” cried Belle as the objects in her room
 tried to comfort her.

“Cheer up, child. It’ll turn out all right in the end,” an enchanted
 teapot told her. “You’ll see.”
