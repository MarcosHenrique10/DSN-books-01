ne cold night, an old beggar woman knocked
 on the door of a beautiful castle. She offered a

young prince a rose in return for shelter. He took one
 look at the woman’s rags and sent her away.

But the old woman was actually a beautiful
 enchantress in disguise. She cast a spell on all who
 lived in the castle and turned the Prince into a
 hideous beast.

With him she left an enchanted mirror and a rose,
 which would bloom until his twenty-first birthday.
 If he could learn to love and be loved before the last
 petal fell, the spell would be broken. If not . . . he
would be a beast forever.
