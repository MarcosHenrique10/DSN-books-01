A pack of hungry wolves growled at Maurice from the darkness.
 He ran away but lost his balance and stumbled down a hill. When
 he looked up, the wolves had him cornered by a gate.

“Help!” he cried, shaking the bars of the gate. “Is someone
 there?”

Just then, the gate creaked open, so he quickly slipped through.
 He made his way to a castle he saw in the distance. Cautiously, he
 opened the front door.
