Belle and her father managed to escape and rushed to the
 castle. They saw Gaston on the roof attacking the Beast, who was
 too sad to defend himself.

“What’s the matter, Beast?” said Gaston. “Too kind and gentle
 to fight back?”

Belle begged Gaston to stop, but he ignored her and stabbed
 the Beast in the back. As the Beast roared in pain, Gaston slipped
 and fell off the roof, never to be seen again.
