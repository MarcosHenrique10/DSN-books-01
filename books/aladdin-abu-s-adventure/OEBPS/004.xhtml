The Magic Carpet swooped through the Agrabah marketplace.

“Abu! Stop covering my eyes!” Aladdin cried as he tried
 to pry the monkey’s arms from around his head. “How can I find a gift for the Genie when I can’t see?”

But Abu didn’t let go. The Magic Carpet flew between the stalls then suddenly stopped short. Aladdin disentangled himself from Abu’s grip as he hopped to the ground.
