“What’s this?” Aladdin asked as he picked up an unusual blue tube.

“Hold it up to the light and look in, and you will see the changing colors of the rainbow,” the merchant replied.

Meanwhile, Abu climbed up the side of the stall. He wrapped his tail around a pole and dangled over Aladdin’s head. Then he grabbed Aladdin’s hat and put it on his own head.

“Give that back!” Aladdin yelled.

“What a clever monkey!” said the
 merchant.

“Sometimes he’s a little too clever,”
 Aladdin replied.

“Perhaps I could buy him?” said the merchant. “For my troupe of traveling acrobats.”

“Abu may be trouble,” said Aladdin. “But he’s not for sale.”
