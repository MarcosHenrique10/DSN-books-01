Although the merchant had wonderful things, nothing was right for Genie, so Aladdin moved on. He searched stand after stand, and finally held up a big yellow robe. “What about this, Abu?” he said. But where was Abu?

 “Abu?” Aladdin called. “Oh, no,” he said to himself. “Abu’s angry because I yelled at him. Now he’s run away . . . Abu, where are you?” he shouted. But there was no answer. Abu had disappeared!
