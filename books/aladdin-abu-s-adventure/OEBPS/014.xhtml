The Genie and Aladdin flew over the desert in search of Abu. They had heard that the travelling acrobats had moved on. But nobody knew where.

“There!” the Genie cried at last, pointing down at a bright red tent.

Aladdin, the Genie, and the Magic Carpet swooped down and took their seats inside the tent. After a moment, the lights went down—and Abu appeared on a high wire!

“Abu! Be careful!” Aladdin immediately called out.
