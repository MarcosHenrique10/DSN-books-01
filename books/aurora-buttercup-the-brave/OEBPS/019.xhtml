Suddenly, Buttercup stopped again.

“What is it this time?” Aurora sighed.

Then she looked ahead . . . and gasped in fright. A huge
 mountain lion was blocking their path!

“Oh, no!” thought Aurora. If Buttercup could be scared by
 a rabbit, what would a fierce mountain lion do to him? But to
 Aurora’s great surprise, Buttercup didn’t try to run.

Instead, he stood his ground and
 snorted angrily, then he struck at
 the lion with his great front hooves.
 Aurora hung on for dear life, still
 afraid. But it seemed that Buttercup
 was feeling very brave!
