“Quit that!” Merryweather cried. “Blue!”

“Pink!” Flora snapped back.

Zap! Zap!

As the two fairies argued, the horse changed from one color to the next.

Aurora watched her new horse change color. That didn’t seem to bother
 him at all. But when a stray leaf fluttered down, he jumped and snorted as
 if a horse-eating dragon were landing on his nose.

Soon it was time to say good-bye. Aurora did her best to ignore the way
 Buttercup jumped at every shadow on the trail home. She had thought
 Buttercup was perfect for her. Now she wasn’t so sure.
