As Aurora and Buttercup neared the castle, a butterfly fluttered past.
 Buttercup’s eyes went wide, and he fearfully jumped back. But this time
 Aurora gently stroked his neck and told him to try to stay calm.

The butterfly fluttered closer . . . and closer . . . until it landed lightly
 on Buttercup’s nose.

“Good boy!” she praised him. “You know, Buttercup, I think we make a
 perfect team!”
