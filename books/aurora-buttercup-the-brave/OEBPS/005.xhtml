“Which horse would you like to ride
 today?” a stable groom asked Princess
 Aurora one morning.

Aurora looked around the royal stables.
 There were so many horses! It was always
 hard to choose.

But then a fine palomino caught
 Aurora’s eye. The former warhorse was by
 far the finest horse she’d ever seen. She rode
 him around the castle courtyard, where he
 marched as if he were the king.
