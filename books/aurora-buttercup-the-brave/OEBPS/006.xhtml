Suddenly the princess had an idea. “From now
 on, I think I’d like to ride this same horse every day.
 What’s his name?” she asked.

“We call him Brutus,” the groom replied.

“That name won’t do,” said Aurora. “I’ll call
 him . . . Buttercup instead.”

The groom and Prince Phillip both looked
 surprised. “Er—are you sure?” the prince asked.

“Do you like your new name, Buttercup?” she
 asked the horse, who happily snorted back.
