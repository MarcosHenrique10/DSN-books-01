When the mountain lion didn’t budge, Buttercup leaped forward and
 pinned its tail to the ground with one hoof. Then Aurora reached out and
 gave it a rap on the nose with her branch.

The lion let out a yowl and yanked its tail free and raced away,
 embarrassed.

“Come along, Buttercup. Let’s go home,” Aurora said, giving the proud
 horse a grateful pat.
