“You just need to be patient with him,” Flora told her.

“Yes, dear, be patient,” Merryweather agreed. Then she moved closer.
 “What a nice coat he has . . . and such shiny hooves. Though he might
 look even nicer if they were blue . . .”

Zap! Merryweather aimed her wand, and just like that, Buttercup’s
 hooves turned blue!
