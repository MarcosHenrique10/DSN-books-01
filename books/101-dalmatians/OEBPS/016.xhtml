One winter evening, Nanny offered to look after the puppies
while Roger and Anita took Pongo and Perdita on a walk. Just as
she put the little ones to bed, two suspicious-looking men came to
the front door.

“Good evening, ma’am. We’re here to inspect the wiring and the
switches,” said the tall one.

Nanny didn’t believe them. “You’re not coming in here! I’ll call the
police!” she cried.

But the two thugs pushed passed Nanny and kidnapped the puppies!
