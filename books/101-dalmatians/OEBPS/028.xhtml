Braving the bitter cold, Pongo and Perdita led the ninety-
nine puppies across the ice and snow back toward London.
It was a long, slow journey, and the puppies grew cold and
tired. Along the way, a friendly cocker spaniel offered them
shelter in a dairy barn so they could warm up.

They were miles from the De Vil mansion by now.
But they weren’t safe yet. Somehow Cruella had followed
their tracks and was right outside!
