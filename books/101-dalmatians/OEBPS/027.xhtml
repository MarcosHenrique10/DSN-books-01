Safe at the barn, Perdita nuzzled Rolly, Lucky,
Penny, Patch, and the rest of her darling puppies.
But there were more than just her own puppies to
look out for now.

Pongo gazed out the window at the snowy countryside.
Horace and Jasper were sure to come after them.
And that meant Cruella would be close behind.

It was risky, but there was only one choice.
“We have to get back to London somehow,” Pongo
declared. “Perdy, we’ll take them home with us.
All of them.”
