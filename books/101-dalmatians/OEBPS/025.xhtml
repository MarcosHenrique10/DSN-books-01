The two crooks chased the puppies through the mansion and
eventually cornered them in the living room. Sergeant Tibs tried
to shield the Dalmatians, but he was too small to protect all
ninety-nine.

Just as Jasper was raising his club to strike, Pongo and Perdita
burst through a window! They snarled and yapped, fighting off
the crooks so the puppies could escape back to the barn. Then
Pongo and Perdita raced away, too.
