Pongo and Perdita were heartbroken. They were certain
Cruella was behind it. But without any proof, there was nothing
the police could do. Pongo realized there was only one hope left.

“Perdy, I’m afraid it’s all up to us,” he said. “There’s the
Twilight Bark. It’s the very fastest way to send news. If our
puppies are anywhere in the city, the London dogs will know.”

So that night on their evening walk, Pongo and Perdita barked
as loudly as they could to alert all the dogs in London. From
backyards to rooftops, the London dogs passed the news along to
try and help Perdita and Pongo find their puppies.
