Perdita and Pongo were so relieved. Roger had stood up to
Cruella and their puppies were safe!

Weeks passed. The puppies all grew healthy and strong. Pongo
smiled every time he thought about their completely different
and loveable personalities.

Rolly was always hungry. Lucky always got into mischief. Penny
and Patch were never apart. And they all loved watching their
hero Thunderbolt’s adventures on television.
