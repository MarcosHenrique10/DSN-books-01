Cruella was furious. Ranting and raving, she slammed her car
into the side of the van. She wanted to drive them off the road!

The moving van teetered on the edge of an embankment. It
was about to tumble over! Out of nowhere, Horace and Jasper
came skidding down the hill. They were trying to catch the van,
too. But they hit Cruella’s car instead!

The villains plummeted down the slope, slipping and sliding
until they crashed at the very bottom. Cruella crawled out of the
wreck and cried, “You fools!”

Pongo and Perdita sighed. They were safe at last.
