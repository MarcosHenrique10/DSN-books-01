Using the Twilight Bark, the Colonel sent word to Pongo and
Perdita. Pongo’s eyes grew wide when he heard that the puppies
were being held prisoner at the De Vil mansion. He and Perdita
left home and headed north to find them.

Perdita was so worried. Cruella had been behind the
kidnapping. Who knew what evil plans that woman had in store?
She hoped they weren’t too late!
