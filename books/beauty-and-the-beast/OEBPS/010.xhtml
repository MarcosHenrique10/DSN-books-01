One cold day Maurice hitched his horse, Phillipe,
to a wagon and set off to show his latest invention at a
faraway fair.

But Maurice read the map wrong and became lost in
a forest. As an icy wind whistled through the trees, he
suddenly heard the howling of wolves! Phillipe bolted,
and Maurice fell to the ground. Trying to escape the
wolves, the frightened man ran deeper and deeper into
the woods.
