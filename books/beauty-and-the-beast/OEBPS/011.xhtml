He came to a castle and stumbled inside. There
he was greeted by Mrs. Potts the teapot, Cogsworth
the clock, and Lumiere the candelabrum, who had all
been servants to the prince. But before he had time to
marvel over these strange creatures, an even stranger one
appeared—the Beast!

When Maurice stared in horror, the Beast howled
angrily. Then he scooped Maurice up and carried him
off to a dungeon.
