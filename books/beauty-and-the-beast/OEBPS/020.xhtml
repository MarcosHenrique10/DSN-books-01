“You can,” the Beast said,
handing her the magic mirror.

Belle gazed into it and saw
Maurice trudging through the
forest. He looked frail and old.
As she watched, he collapsed in
a heap.

“I must go to him!” Belle
cried. “He might be dying!”

“I release you,” the Beast said
sadly. “But take the mirror. Then
you will always have a way to
look back and remember me.”
