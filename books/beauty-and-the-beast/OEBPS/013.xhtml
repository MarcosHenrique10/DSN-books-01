When they reached the castle, Belle burst inside and
searched frantically for her father. The enchanted objects
led her to the dungeon, but just as she found Maurice,
the Beast appeared. Belle let out a terrified gasp at the
sight of the hideous creature.

She begged the Beast to free her father. When he
refused, she bravely offered to take Maurice’s place.

“No, Belle!” Maurice cried, but the Beast agreed to
the exchange.
