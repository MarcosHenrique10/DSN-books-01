The Beast roared in pain. Backing away from the
wounded Beast, Gaston lost his footing and fell off the
roof into the fog below.

Belle rushed to the Beast’s side.

“You came back,” the Beast said weakly. “At least I
can see you one last time.”

“No! No!” Belle said, sobbing as she kissed his
cheek. “Please don’t die. . . . I love you.”
