The townspeople looked at the Beast in the magic mirror
and grew frightened.

“We must hunt down this savage animal!” Gaston cried.

After locking Belle and her father in the cellar of the
cottage, the villagers rode off to storm the Beast’s castle.

Luckily, little Chip had stowed away in Belle’s saddlebag.
After the villagers were gone, he used Maurice’s latest
invention to release Belle and Maurice from the cellar.
