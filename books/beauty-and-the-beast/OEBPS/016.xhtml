Her heart pounding, Belle ran out of the castle, mounted
Phillipe, and galloped off into the night. But a pack of wolves
soon had them surrounded. Belle was helpless.

Suddenly the Beast was there, throwing the wolves aside.
Belle heard terrible snarling and howling as the Beast and the
wolves battled for their lives. At last the wolves ran off into
the woods, but the Beast lay in the snow, badly injured.
