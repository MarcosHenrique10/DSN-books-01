“Hello, Pooh,” Christopher Robin said as he approached the bear.

“Hello, Christopher Robin.” Pooh Bear smiled. The balloon bobbed,
 and Christopher Robin waved at Pooh.

He tried to find the right words. “I’m so terribly sorry, Pooh.
 I never should have yelled.”

“But I am a bear of very little brain,” Pooh replied.

Christopher Robin sat beside him.

“YOU ARE, I THINK,

A BEAR OF

VERY BIG

HEART.”
