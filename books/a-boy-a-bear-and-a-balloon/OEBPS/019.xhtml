“Hello, Heffalump,” Eeyore said
 once they were out of the river.

Christopher Robin helped him dry off. “I’m not a Heffalump, I’m
 Christopher Robin, the one who used to try and cheer you up.”

Eeyore stared at him. “I don’t remember being cheery.”

Christopher Robin decided to move on to a different topic. “How did
 you end up in the water?” he asked.

“Woke up. Windy. House blew down. Fell in the river.
 Can’t swim—”

A loud Heffalump growl interrupted them.
