Christopher Robin thought the Hundred-Acre Wood seemed
 different, too. “Pooh, was it always this gloomy?” he asked,
 gripping his case of Important Things. “We don’t seem to be
 getting anywhere.”

“Normally,” Pooh mused, “I just wait for SOMEWHERE to
 come to me. We could stop until this mist is . . . mistless.”

But Christopher Robin decided he could get them unlost.
