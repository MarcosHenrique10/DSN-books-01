They trudged on and soon came
 across an alarming warning.

“Oh, bother,” said Pooh.

“There are no such things as
 Heffalumps and Woozles,” said
 Christopher Robin.

“Of course there are,” Pooh replied.
 “Didn’t you see the sign?”

Luckily, the pair didn’t meet any
 Heffalumps or Woozles . . .
 or even any Jagulars. But they
 did come across some familiar
 tracks.

“Those are our footsteps!”
 Christopher Robin cried.
 “We just went in a circle!”
