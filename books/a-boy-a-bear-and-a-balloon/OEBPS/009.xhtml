Just then, Christopher Robin’s case of
 Important Things flew open. Sheets
 of paper twirled through the sky.

he exclaimed.

Pooh offered his balloon, for no
 one could be un-cheered with
 a balloon.

Yet Christopher Robin was still not
 cheered. “There’s more to life than
 balloons and honey!” he yelled. “I’m
 not a child. I’m an adult now!”

“But you’re Christopher Robin,”
 Pooh said.

“No,” Christopher Robin replied.
 “I’m not how you remember me.”
