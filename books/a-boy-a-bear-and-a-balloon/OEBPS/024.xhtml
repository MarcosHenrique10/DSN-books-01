Christopher Robin and Eeyore
 hurried through the Wood,
 from one haycorn to the next,
 calling out for their friends.

After a little while, they heard a
 squeaky voice: “Who is it? Who’s
 th-th-there?”

“It’s just me,” Eeyore replied.

Little Piglet breathed a sigh of
 relief as he stepped out from behind
 a tree. “Eeyore, thank goodness
 it’s you.”

“OH, AND A

HEFFALUMP.”

Eeyore nodded toward
 Christopher Robin.

Piglet screamed, running off, despite
 Christopher Robin’s protests.
