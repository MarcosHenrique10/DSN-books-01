Luckily, the Magic
 Carpet freed itself and
 caught Aladdin and Abu before
 they hit the ground. Above, the
 tiger head gave a final roar and
 sank back into the sand.

“We’re trapped,” Aladdin
 said angrily.

But Abu grinned at Aladdin and
 pulled out the magic lamp.

“Why, you hairy little thief!” Aladdin said, patting
 Abu. “Hey—I think there’s something written here . . .”
 Aladdin rubbed the side of the lamp.
