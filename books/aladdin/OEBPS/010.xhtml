Meanwhile, Jasmine was enjoying her newfound
 freedom in the marketplace. When she saw a hungry little
 boy, she plucked a piece of fruit from a nearby fruit stand
 and gave it to him. Used to life in the palace, she didn’t
 know she needed to pay for it.

“No one steals from my cart. Thief!”
 shouted the vendor, taking out his sword.

Luckily, Aladdin intervened before
 anyone got hurt. Taking Jasmine’s hand,
 Aladdin led her through the marketplace
 and away from the angry vendor.
