Jafar, the Sultan’s trusted advisor, said he could change
 Jasmine’s mind about getting married. “But,” Jafar said,
 eyeing the Sultan’s ring, “it would require the use of the
 Mystic Blue Diamond.” Jafar hypnotized the Sultan with
 his snakelike staff and stole the ring.

He took the Diamond to his secret chamber to activate
 the Sands of Time. “Reveal to me the one who can enter
 the Cave,” he commanded. The Sands showed Aladdin!
 Jafar ordered his guards to bring Aladdin to the palace
 at once.
