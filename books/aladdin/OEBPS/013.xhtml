They escaped from the dungeon through a hidden exit.
 The old man brought Aladdin and Abu to the entrance to
 the Cave of Wonders. The tiger head roared open, and a staircase made of sand appeared inside its jaws.

“Who disturbs my slumber?” the tiger head thundered.

“It is I, Aladdin,” Aladdin said faintly.

“Proceed,” roared the tiger. “Touch nothing but the lamp.”
