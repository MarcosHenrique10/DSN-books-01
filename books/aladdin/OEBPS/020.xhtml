Aladdin grabbed onto a ledge to keep from
 falling back into the Cave. “Give me your hand!”
 Aladdin cried out to the old man.

“First give me the lamp!” the old man shouted.

Aladdin gave him the lamp, but the old man
 laughed as he took off his disguise. It was really
 Jafar! He let Aladdin and Abu fall back into the
 depths of the Cave.
