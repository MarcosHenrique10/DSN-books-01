Thinking of Princess Jasmine,
Aladdin made his first wish. “I wish
for you to make me a prince!”

In a flash, Aladdin was riding into
 Agrabah in style: sword twirlers,
 acrobats, and a menagerie of animals
 escorted him down the city streets.

“Make way for Prince Ali!” the
 parade sang on its way to the palace.
