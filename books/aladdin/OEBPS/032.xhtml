Aladdin had an idea. “Face it, Jafar—
you’re still just second best!” he said,
 hinting at the Genie’s great power.

Jafar made his final wish. “I wish
 to be an all-powerful genie!” he said,
 and another magic lamp appeared.

“You want to be a genie, you got
 it!” Aladdin said, holding up Jafar’s
 lamp. Jafar was sucked into the lamp,
 imprisoned forever!
