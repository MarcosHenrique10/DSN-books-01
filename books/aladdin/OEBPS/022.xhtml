The lamp began to rattle in Aladdin’s hands,
 shooting out a stream of pink and purple smoke.
 Then—poof—a genie emerged!

“Here for your wish fulfillment!” said the Genie.
 “So what’ll it be, Master?”

Aladdin didn’t want to use any wishes yet, so he tried
 to trick the Genie into helping them escape. “He probably
 can’t even get us out of this cave,” he said under his breath.

“Excuse me?” the Genie gasped. “Are you lookin’ at me?
 Did you rub my lamp?” Instantly, they were out of the
 Cave and at a desert oasis.
