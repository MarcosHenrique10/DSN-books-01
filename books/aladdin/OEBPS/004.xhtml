ONE NIGHT, IN THE ARABIAN DESERT, the evil sorcerer
 Jafar put two halves of an enchanted scarab together.
 It flew out of his hands and into the sand, where a giant
 tiger head appeared.

“After all my years of searching . . . the Cave of
 Wonders!” Jafar cried in awe. He was so close to finding
 the magic lamp. Suddenly, the tiger head began to speak.
 It told Jafar that only a person of great worth could enter
 the Cave and retrieve the lamp. “I must find this one,”
 Jafar said, “this . . . Diamond in the Rough.”
