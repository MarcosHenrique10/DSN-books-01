Aladdin rushed back to the palace. He needed to show
 Jasmine and the Sultan who Jafar really was! But when
 he arrived at the palace to expose the evil sorcerer, Jafar’s
 parrot, Iago, stole the lamp and gave it to Jafar!

Jafar rubbed the lamp, summoning the Genie. “I wish
to rule as sultan!” Jafar cried, making his first wish as the
 Genie’s new master.
