The next morning, in the marketplace of Agrabah, a
 boy named Aladdin and his pet monkey, Abu, were fleeing
 from palace guards. They had stolen a loaf of bread.

“Stop, street rat!” called the guards as Aladdin glided
 over rooftops and through alleyways to escape them.

“Gotta eat to live! Gotta steal to eat!” Aladdin called
 back as he made his way out of sight.
