Back at Aladdin’s rooftop home, they talked about how
 they both felt trapped in their worlds. They looked into
 each other’s eyes, and Aladdin leaned in to kiss her—

“Here you are, street rat!” shouted a guard.

“Unhand him, by order of the princess!” Jasmine yelled,
 pulling off her hood.

“The princess?” gasped Aladdin, surprised by who
 she was.

“I would, Princess, except my orders come from Jafar,”
 the guard responded as he hauled Aladdin away.
