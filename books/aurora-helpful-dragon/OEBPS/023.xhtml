Everyone tried to see Crackle at the top of the tower, but
 the storm was too dark and strong. Suddenly, they saw huge
 flames. They were coming from little Crackle! Gold and red
 light flashed in the sky above the watchtower.

Again and again, Crackle blew his flames until, at last,
 Phillip shouted, “I see King Stefan and the Queen! They’re
 almost here!”
