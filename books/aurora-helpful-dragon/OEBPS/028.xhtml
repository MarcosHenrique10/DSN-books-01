When Aurora finds a baby dragon in the forest,
she decides to bring him back to the castle. There’s
just one problem: Crackle is worried that no one
will like him. The little dragon tries to act like the
other castle animals—a cat, a dog, a bird, even a
fish—but something goes wrong every time! Can
Aurora convince Crackle that he’s perfect just the
way he is, before someone gets hurt?
