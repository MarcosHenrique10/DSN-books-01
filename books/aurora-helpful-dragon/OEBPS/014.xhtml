When Aurora saw Crackle creep
 from the stable she carried him into the
 castle and cuddled him on a window
 seat. A bird was singing outside.
 Crackle’s ears perked up and his eyes
 shone hopefully.

“LAAAlaagrr!” he bellowed.

King Hubert heard the racket and
 rushed into the room.

“Oh, my, my, my! How did a
 dragon get in here?” he blustered.
