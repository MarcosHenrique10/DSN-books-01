Crackle looked sad for a moment. Then he saw the kitten playing
 with a ball of yarn from the workbasket, and his eyes lit up. He
 snatched a ball of yarn with his mouth. Whoosh!—it caught fire.
 Merryweather put the fire out with her wand.

“Oh, Crackle,” Aurora said gently. “You’re not a kitten. You’re
 a dragon.”

Crackle’s lower lip trembled.
