“Crackle, you’re not a fish!” Aurora exclaimed as she pulled
 Crackle from the pool. “You’re not a kitten or a dog or a bird,
 either. You’re a dragon!”

Tears rolled down Crackle’s face. “Grrgrrgrr,” he sobbed.
 Suddenly, Aurora understood.

“Do you think no one will like you because you’re a dragon?”
 she asked.

Crackle nodded and whimpered sadly.
