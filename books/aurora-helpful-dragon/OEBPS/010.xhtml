Crackle listened to the cute kitten purring. Crackle
 scrunched up his mouth and closed his eyes.

“Purrgrr, purrgrr!” Crackle tried to purr. Clouds of
 smoke puffed from his nose and mouth.

“Aachoo! Aachooooie! Ah-ah-ah-CHOO!” The fairies
 sneezed so hard that they fluttered backward.

“Please–Achoo!–stop trying to purr!” Fauna exclaimed.
