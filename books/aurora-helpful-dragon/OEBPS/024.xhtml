Everyone hurried to greet the visiting royals.

“The tower light saved us!” King Stefan
 exclaimed. “I need one like it!”

At that moment Crackle flew happily to
 join in the fun.

“Well, there he is! Our new tower light,”
 King Hubert said with a laugh.

“A dragon?” King Stefan asked. “But
 dragons are danger—”

“Not Crackle,” Aurora interrupted.
 “He’s a brave and helpful dragon!”
