Just then, Crackle saw Phillip leading the horses into the
 stables. A dog followed Phillip, barking and wagging its tail.
 Crackle wagged his tail and ran to the stables, too.

“Woofgrr, woofgrr,” he tried to bark. Flames shot from
 his mouth and caught some straw on fire. Phillip poured
 water on the burning straw.

“You’re not a dog,” he said kindly, shooing Crackle away.
