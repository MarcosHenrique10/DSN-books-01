That night at the ball, Crackle lit the candles, warmed
 food, and kept the fireplace blazing. King Hubert and the
 fairies were so pleased that they took turns scratching
 Crackle beneath his chin.

As Prince Phillip and Aurora danced, Crackle trotted
 beside them. Outside, it was cold and stormy. But inside,
 everyone was happy and warm—especially Crackle the
 helpful dragon.
