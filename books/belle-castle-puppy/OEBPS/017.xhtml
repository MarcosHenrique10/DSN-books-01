“Puppy! Ottoman!” Belle called as she and Lumiere
 searched. Something rustled in the bushes. Yellow eyes
 gleamed at them.

“What is that?” Lumiere whispered.

“I hope it’s just squirrels,” Belle answered.

“They must be very big squirrels with very big
 eyes,” Lumiere replied.

Belle picked up a large stick. Then she and
 Lumiere walked on, calling and calling.
