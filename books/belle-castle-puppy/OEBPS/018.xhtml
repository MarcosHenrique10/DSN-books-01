Suddenly, they heard ferocious barking and
 snarling nearby. Belle ran toward the sound
 and stumbled into a clearing. The ottoman
 and the puppy stood under an enormous
 tree. Snarling wolves circled them. But the
 puppy was growling and snapping.

“He’s protecting Ottoman!” Lumiere
 exclaimed.

“He’s too small to stop those wolves for
 long,” Belle answered. “He needs help!”
