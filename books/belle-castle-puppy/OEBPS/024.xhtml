Suddenly, the Beast crashed into the clearing.
 The wolves scattered, yelping with fear.

The danger had passed. But the puppy’s
 nose and ears were singed and sore.

“He and Ottoman tried to save me!” Belle
 said.

“They are brave little fellows,” the Beast
 answered. Cradling the puppy in one arm
 and the ottoman in the other, he led Belle
 to the castle.
