“You’re going to do what?” Flounder exclaimed. “Triple-banded sea oysters live in Blackbeard’s Trench! You’ll have to cross the Barbed Sea Kelp Forest.”

Ariel shrugged. “Adella needs help. Someone has to get the cure. Besides, I’ve been in kelp forests before.”

“Not like this one,” Flounder said. “This kelp is huge! There’s a riptide! Each strand of sea kelp is covered in thorns! And the spaces are tiny!”

“Don’t be such a guppy,” Ariel said. “Are you coming?” Flounder didn’t like being called a guppy. Together, he and Ariel headed off to find the purple pearl.
