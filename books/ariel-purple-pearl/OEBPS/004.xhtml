“Aaaaaaaah!” a voice wailed. Ariel stopped short. It was her sister Adella, and she sounded upset! Ariel swam closer and peeked into the room. Her other sister, Aquata, was with her.

“You can’t let anyone see you. You have a bad case of the Bubbles!” Aquata said to Adella.

“The Bubbles?” Adella asked. Her face was covered with pink and green polka dots!
