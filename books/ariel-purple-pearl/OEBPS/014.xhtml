It was hard to believe they would ever eat a mermaid. “Maybe it’s just a myth,” Ariel said. “I’ll just swim in and—”

“No!” Flounder grabbed Ariel’s tail. “We’ve got to be sure.” He took a branch of driftwood in his mouth and tossed it into the nearest oyster’s shell.

SNAP! The shell chomped the driftwood in half!

Maybe there was something in Ariel’s collection that could help. She emptied out her bag. Tucked in a corner of the bag was a long feather with a pointy tip.
