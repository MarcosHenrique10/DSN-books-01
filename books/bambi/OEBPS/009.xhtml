So when a skunk rose out of the flower patch Bambi
had discovered, Bambi proudly said, “Flower.”

Thumper laughed so hard he rolled on the ground.
“No, no, no! That’s not a flower! He’s a little—”

“Oh, that’s all right,” the skunk quickly interrupted.
“He can call me a flower if he wants to. I don’t mind.”

It didn’t take long for Bambi, Thumper, and Flower
to become great friends.
