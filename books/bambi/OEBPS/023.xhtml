“My, my—how you’ve changed!” said Friend Owl
when he saw how Bambi and the others had grown.
Friend Owl told his young friends it was only a matter
of time before they would become “twitterpated.”

Bambi, Flower, and Thumper looked confused, so
Friend Owl explained. “You begin to get weak in the
knees. Your head’s in a whirl. And then you feel light as a
feather, and before you know it, you’re walking on air!”

Bambi swore it would never happen to him.
