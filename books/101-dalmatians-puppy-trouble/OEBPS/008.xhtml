At the playground, the puppies
 scampered off to explore. Patch and
 Pepper began digging in a sandbox.
 Rolly found a rope to chew. Nanny tried
 to keep an eye on all of them—but that
 was hard to do!

When she went to retrieve Penny, who had gotten
 tangled in some vines, Lucky suddenly spotted a butterfly.
 He tried to pounce on it, but it flew off toward a slide.
