Lucky raced across the street. Honk!
A car swerved and drove through a
 puddle, splashing mud all over him. But
 Lucky kept running . . . and running . . . 
 until he reached the playground, at last.

Nanny was at the park fence, trying
 to count all the puppies, but they kept
 running around.

“Oh, I give up,” she finally said.

Lucky barked and barked, scratching
 eagerly at the gate.

Nanny looked down. “Why, hello,
 little pup,” she said as Lucky wagged his
  tail. “Too bad you can’t come with us.
       But you’re not a Dalmatian. You
            should find your own family.
                I’m sure they’re worried
                     about you.”
