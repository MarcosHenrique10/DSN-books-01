When Lucky reached the Radcliffe’s house, he spotted Nanny outside.

“My goodness,” Nanny said as he ran past her. “Where did you come from?”

Lucky ran straight to his sleeping basket and curled up with the other
 puppies and yawned.

When Pongo and Perdita came home, they found Lucky nestled in the basket,
 sound asleep.

“You see?” Pongo whispered to Perdita. “I told you nothing would go wrong.”
