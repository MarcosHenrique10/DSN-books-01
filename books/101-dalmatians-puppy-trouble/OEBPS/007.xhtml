The puppies couldn’t wait to get outside and chase each other and
 play with butterflies and roll around in the dirt. They ran up to Nanny,
 who was watering some plants, with leashes in their mouths.

“Oh, dear,” said Nanny, looking into their big, hopeful eyes. “I suppose
 puppies do need a walk now and then.” And so when all fifteen puppies
 were on their leashes, she led them out to the sidewalk.
