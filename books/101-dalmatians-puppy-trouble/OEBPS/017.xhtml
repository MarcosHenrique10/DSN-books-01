Lucky was confused, but then he saw his
 reflection in a puddle on the ground. He was
 covered with dirt! Nanny wouldn’t recognize
 him unless he washed off all the mud.

He saw some children playing in a fountain
 and ran to jump in, too. Once all the dirt was off,
 Lucky shook out his fur.

“You got my newspaper wet!” a man on a
 bench yelled angrily as Lucky raced home.
