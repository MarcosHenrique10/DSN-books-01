“We won’t be gone long,” Pongo told Perdita. The two
 Dalmatians were joining their human pets, Roger and Anita
 Radcliffe, for a picnic. It was a beautiful summer day in London,
 and Pongo couldn’t wait to get to the park.

“I’m not sure we should leave the puppies,” said Perdita.
 “Will Nanny be able to handle all fifteen of them by herself?”

Nanny was the Radcliffe’s housekeeper, and she took
 excellent care of all the dogs.

Pongo looked at the puppies curled up peacefully in their
 sleeping basket. “What could possibly go wrong?” he asked.

Perdita nodded, and tried to forget her worries as she
 followed Pongo out into the sunshine.
