She put a bonnet on Lucky’s head and
 plopped him into the carriage.

“Grr,” Lucky growled. He didn’t like
 being a doll, and besides, he wanted to
 get back to his family.

Suddenly, the girl stopped. “A
 button!” she cried, bending over,
 and Lucky saw his chance. He
 jumped out of the carriage
 and took off. At the end of
 the block he heard barking!
 His brothers and sisters
 were close!
