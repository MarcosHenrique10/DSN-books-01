After the show ends, it’s time for the
puppies to go to sleep so Pongo and Perdita
can go on a walk with their humans.

“Come along, children,” said
Pongo one evening.

“I’m hungry, mother,”
whined Rolly, the pudgy pup.

“Can’t we stay up a bit
longer?” pleaded Patch.

“It’s time for sleep now,
my dears,” Perdita replied.
