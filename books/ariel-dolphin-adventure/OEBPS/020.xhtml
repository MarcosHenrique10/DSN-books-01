“What has happened here?” King Triton roared.

Eric looked down humbly. “It is entirely my fault, sir,” he explained.
 “I built this wall to make a nice place for Ariel to visit her friends. I was wrong.”

The king of the seas glared at Eric. Then, with a hint of a smile, he added,
 “Well, you are human, after all.”

With a wave of his trident, King Triton called to all the dolphins and they
 quickly found the baby dolphin’s mother! Frantically, she tried to get into
 the lagoon.

“Oh, dear!” Ariel exclaimed. “The gate won’t open! She can’t get in!”

Eric looked at King Triton. “Do you mind?”

“Not at all,” the king replied. “Swim back, everyone!” He raised his
trident and blasted down the wall.
