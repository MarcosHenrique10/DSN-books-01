Eric felt terrible. He now understood that he had made a
 bad decision by closing in the lagoon. He followed Ariel into the
 stormy night, ready to help in any way he could.

When they arrived at the lagoon, Flounder was trying to calm
 the frightened baby dolphin.

“Go to him, Eric,” Ariel said gently. “He feels safe with you.”
 Ariel looked into her prince’s eyes, letting him know that she
 trusted him with her sea friends.
