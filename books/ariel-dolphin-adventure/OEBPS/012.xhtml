Later that night Ariel awoke to a loud clap of thunder. From the
 safety of the palace, she saw terrible waves crashing to the shore.

“Ariel?” Eric asked. “Are you worried about that baby dolphin?”

“Oh, Eric, I am. He must be terrified,” she replied, shuddering.
 “We need to go to him. And, Eric? I need to ask my father for help.”
