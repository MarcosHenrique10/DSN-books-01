Still, Belle did not give up. They continued on to a wide, open
meadow.

“You know,” Belle said, “I bet a good gallop would do the
trick.” She leaned forward and snapped the reins, giving Phillipe’s
sides a firm nudge with her heels.

As if to tell her, “Wrong again,” Phillipe stopped, leaned
down, and nibbled at the clover.

“Oh, Phillipe,” Belle said in despair. “I just don’t know what
else to do!”
