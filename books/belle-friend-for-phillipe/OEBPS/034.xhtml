As they made their way
through the meadow, Belle found
herself wishing Phillipe had a
horse friend at the castle. “If only
there was a way—” Belle began.

She was interrupted by the
sound of hooves behind them.
Belle turned around.

“Well, look at that, Phillipe!”
she exclaimed. “It’s your new
friend.”

The horse who had played
with Phillipe all afternoon was
following them home.

Belle and Phillipe
slowed their pace,
and the shy horse
drew closer . . .
