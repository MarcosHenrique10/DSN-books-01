Phillipe was not as happy as Belle had hoped he would be. He
sniffed at the bunch of carrots. But when Belle offered him one,
Phillipe gently butted her hands away.

“Is something wrong?” Belle asked, alarmed. Phillipe was always
hungry.

Phillipe hung his head and sighed a little sigh. There was something wrong, all right, Belle thought. Phillipe was the gloomiest horse she
had ever seen!
