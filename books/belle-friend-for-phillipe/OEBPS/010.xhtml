“Sacre bleu!” cried Lumiere when he, Cogsworth, and Chip saw
all the books. “What are you doing, Princess?”

“I want to cheer up Phillipe,” Belle explained. “I hoped I’d find
the answer in one of these books . . . but I’m not having much luck.”

“Ah!” said Lumiere, “before you came and freed us from the enchantment, we were often sad.”
