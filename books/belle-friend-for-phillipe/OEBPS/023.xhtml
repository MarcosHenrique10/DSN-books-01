Belle led Phillipe to the edge of the forest where the royal orchards
began. The sight of all the delicious fruit gave Belle an idea.

“Would you like an apple?” Belle asked. “Go ahead and choose one!”

Following Belle’s suggestion, Phillipe wandered from tree to tree,
eyeing each apple and even sniffing some. But soon his head was hanging,
and his steps were slow and heavy. It was clear his heart wasn’t in it.
