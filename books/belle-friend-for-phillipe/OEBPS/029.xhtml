At last they emerged from the trees . . . into a clearing filled
with wild, beautiful horses! Belle and Phillipe stared at the herd
before them. Then Phillipe whinnied, and several of the wild
horses answered him.

Finally, Belle realized what Phillipe had wanted. Not a fancy
stall, or fine music, or a bubble bath. Not an apple or a run.
What Phillipe had wanted was to be with other horses!
