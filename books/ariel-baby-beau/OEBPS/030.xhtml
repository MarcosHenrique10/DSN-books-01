“I’m so sorry, Your Majesties,” Ariel told the visitors. “Beau
doesn’t know any better. This is all my fault.”

But the king and queen didn’t seem very upset. “It’s quite
all right, my dear,” the queen said. “No harm done.”

“Indeed,” the king agreed. “We love horses. In fact, that
reminds me— we brought you two a gift. Please come outside.”

Ariel and Eric exchanged a glance. A gift? What could it be?
They followed the visitors out of the castle. As usual, Beau
tagged along.
