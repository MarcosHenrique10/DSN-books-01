But the castle didn’t stay peaceful for long. Suddenly, Beau burst in
and galloped through the throne room— almost knocking over the guests!

“Oh, dear!” Ariel cried in alarm. “Beau, no!”

She could tell that Beau didn’t mean any harm. But all at once, Ariel
realized what it must look like to the guests. How many castles allowed a
horse to play in their throne room? Beau was nearly grown up now. He
was too big to stay inside.
