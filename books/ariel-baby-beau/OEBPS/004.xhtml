Ariel’s new life on land was always full of surprises. One day, Eric
told her he had something to show her, and he led her out to the stable.

“Surprise!” he exclaimed. Standing there was a cute little foal with
long legs, big eyes, and the sweetest expression.

Ariel gasped. “It’s a baby horse!” she cried. “Oh, he’s adorable!”

“He’s all yours,” Eric said with a smile. “His name is Beau.”
