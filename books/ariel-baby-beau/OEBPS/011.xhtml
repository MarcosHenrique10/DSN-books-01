Grimsby and the other servants were quite surprised when Ariel led
Beau into the castle. “Oh, dear,” Grimsby said. “A horse in the house?”

“It’s just for tonight,” Ariel said with a smile. “Don’t worry, he
won’t be any trouble at all.”

She made Beau his own cozy bed near the fireplace. Soon he was all
tucked in.

“Good night, little Beau.” Ariel gave him a kiss on the nose. “Sleep
tight!”
