The next morning dawned cool and rainy. Ariel looked out the
window and then led Beau into the dining hall.

“This is no weather for baby horses,” she told Eric. “I think Beau
had better stay inside and have breakfast with us.”

Soon the cook had set another place for the hungry foal. Ariel,
Beau, Eric, and Eric’s dog, Max, all enjoyed their meals while the rain
pounded against the windows.
